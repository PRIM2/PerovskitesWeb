Extended Tolerance Factor


The extended formula for Goldschmidt's tolerance factor is: \[ \alpha = \frac{r_{A,\text{eff}} + r_{X,\text{eff}}}{\sqrt{2} \left( r_B + 0.5 h_{X,\text{eff}} \right)} \] where:

  • \(r_{A,\text{eff}}\) effective radius of cation A
  • \(r_B\) radius of cation B
  • \(r_{X,\text{eff}}\) effective radius of anion
  • \(h_{X,\text{eff}}\) cylinder length of anion
The globularity of cation A should be considered for greater accuracy.
